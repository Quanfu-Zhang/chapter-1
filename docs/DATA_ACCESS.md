# Data access and confidentiality

The microdata used in this study cannot be redistributed through GitHub.

The primary data are Stats NZ's Household Economic Survey (HES), linked administrative address histories, and other IDI-held administrative sources used to reconstruct pre-earthquake residence. The research was conducted under approved IDI project **MAA2024-54**.

## Controlled-environment use

Restricted-data components are intended to be executed only within the controlled Stats NZ Integrated Data Infrastructure (IDI) environment by researchers approved for the relevant research project, and in accordance with Stats NZ access, security, confidentiality, and output-checking requirements.

The restricted-data scripts document the logic of the study so that readers can understand the implementation and, where appropriate, adapt similar methods within an appropriately approved research environment.

## Confidentiality

No row-level microdata, confidential identifiers, project extracts, credentials, or restricted outputs should be committed to this repository.

The public repository contains code and documentation only. Any execution against IDI data must follow the relevant Stats NZ approval and requirements.

## Stats NZ disclaimer

Access to the data used in this study was provided by Stats NZ under conditions designed to give effect to the security and confidentiality provisions of the Data and Statistics Act 2022. The results are the work of the authors, not Stats NZ or individual data suppliers.

Results created from the Integrated Data Infrastructure are not official statistics and are produced for research purposes from data managed by Stats NZ.
